GBSC (Global Business Services Center) – Financial Systems Team owns and operates strategic financial applications. As a summer intern, I assisted the team in many activities.

I used Alteryx to extract and blend Supplier data, Sales and Procurement Contracts data from Ariba and Oracle. I performed data analysis and data cleanup activities using Excel and  created dashboards in Tableau.

I designed and implemented an intake form using Quatrics, exporting functionalities like date-picker using JavaScript and data type formatting with the regular expression.

In the [Innovation challenge](<{% post_url projects/2017-07-27-bgc-mobile-app %}>), I designed and implemented a prototype for Boys and Girls Club based on requirements.
