## Configuration

When launching the application, the tester will first see the setting window popping up. The tester needs to enter the name for Player Light, Player Dark, height and width.

The tester can change any field. ~~Note that all the fields should be filled and valid, and the height and the width must exceed 8; otherwise, the game is not allowed to start.~~ 👈 Not implemented, so please be kind and enter valid stuff.

Then, the tester can select either to use the standard chess pieces or the customized ones.

## Layout

After selecting the chess set, the tester will see the following GUI, which consists of two parts: the chess board with pieces on the left, and the info panel on the right.

There are five components in the info panel, including

-   the header panel
-   the player panel of names, `Pawn` icons indicating the color, and scores, with underline showing the active player
-   the console panel
-   the history panel with `Undo`, left `<<`, right `>>`, `Restart` and `Forfeit` buttons, and with the current step and the total step
-   the footer panel

![Standard, default](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/1_initial_8_standard.png)

## Movements

The tester can try to move all pieces in both colors to check all valid moves, and he or she can see the effect from the updated board and the change of the active player with the purple underline.

The following rules are from [Chess - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess).

### Pawn

> The pawn can move forward to the unoccupied square immediately in front of it on the same file, or on its first move it can advance two squares along the same file, provided both squares are unoccupied (black dots in the diagram); or the pawn can capture an opponent's piece on a square diagonally in front of it on an adjacent file, by moving to that square (black "x"s). ~~A pawn has two special moves: the en passant capture and promotion.~~

For example, the tester, as the `Lilian`, takes the first move. When the tester clicks on a `lightPawn`, that piece turns white, and possible destinations are indicated. The tester can move one or two steps forward. The console shows the selected position and the number of possible destinations.

![Light Pawn 1](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/2_Pawn_Light_1.png)

After the tester moves that piece. The console shows the current move.

![Light Pawn 2](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/2_Pawn_Light_2.png)

Any move enables the `Undo` and `<<` buttons, while the `>>` button remains disabled. Now the tester, as `Daniel`, can move a `darkPawn` two steps forward as well.

![Dark Pawn](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/2_Pawn_Dark.png)

A `Pawn` cannot go straight if there is a piece in the front.

### Knight

> The knight moves to any of the closest squares that are not on the same rank, file, or diagonal, thus the move forms an "L"-shape: two squares vertically and one square horizontally, or two squares horizontally and one square vertically. ### Queen

### Invalid Movements

Similarly, the tester can test invalid moves, including choosing invalid destinations or picking the wrong color. The GUI will not let the tester select invalid pieces or targets. The board, as well as the purple underline, remained unchanged.

The console shows different messages based on different invalid moves.

#### Invalid Position

Selection is invalid if the user clicks an empty grid or selects the other player's pieces.

#### Let `King` be under check

A player cannot let his or her `King` be under check. The step is not included in the possible steps as well. For example, the `darkKing` cannot move to `(2, 4)` in this case, since there is a `lightQueen` at `(0, 2)`

## Undo & History

The tester can test the `Undo` button. Each time the button is clicked, the board and the player panel will go back one stage. The current and total steps decrease until reaching 0.

| Undo Once                                                         | Undo Three Times                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Undo Once](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/5_Undo.png) | ![Undo Three Times](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/5_Undo3.png) |

At any time, the tester can click on the `<<` button and the `>>` button to see the history. If there is at least one move and it is at the latest step, the `>>` button is disabled; if it is at the initial state, the `<<` button is disabled. Note that the `Undo` button is disabled and no current player indicated if the status is not at the latest.

The tester should click these two buttons for multiple times to discover potential bugs in the implementation.

| Left                                                         | Left 3 Times                                                  | Right 3 Times                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Left](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/6_Left.png) | ![Left](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/6_Left3.png) | ![Right](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/6_Right3.png) |

The tester should also check the behavior if he or she clicks the `Undo` or the `<<` button when having a piece selected. The indications of the current selection (the selected piece, possible destinations) should be gone, i.e., there is no white color on the board.

## Check and Ending Condition

<iframe width="640" height="360" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/U3X2PWjrvZE" frameborder="0" gesture="media" allowfullscreen></iframe>

If the game has not ended, but a `King` is under check after a move, the GUI should have an appropriate prompt.

![](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/7_Check.png)

The tester can follow some online examples for [checkmate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fool%27s_mate#/media/File:Fools_mate_animation.gif) and [stalemate](https://www.chess.com/article/view/the-shortest-stalemate-possible) to quickly reach those conditions. But the tester is welcomed to come up with his or her case as well :)

If there is a checkmate or stalemate, the GUI should let the tester know and end the game. There is no more active player, and the undo button is disabled, but the tester can still see the history.

| Checkmate                                                              | Stalemate                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![Checkmate](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/8_Checkmate.png) | ![Stalemate](/assets/posts/2018-10-05-cs242-project-1/9_Stalemate.png) |

On the other hand, if the game has not ended, there should not be any checkmate or stalemate alert.

## Restart & Forfeit

The tester can test the `Restart` button at any time, such as the end of a game or during a game, and see the popped up window.

-   If the tester clicks `No` nothing will happened
-   If the tester clicks `Yes`, the game will start over, but the scores are kept.

If the tester clicks `Forfeit`, the other player gets one point.
